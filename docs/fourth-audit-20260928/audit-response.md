# Response to the fourth manuscript audit

28 September 2026. This revision changes the argument and presentation, not the recorded experiments or their primary analyses. The previous sources, reports, PDF, and inventory are preserved in `prerevision-source-and-paper.tar.gz`. No new workload was executed.

## Decisions and corrections

| Audit concern | Revision and evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Predictable mechanisms displaced the useful measurements. | The title is now **Trace Sampling at the Collector Boundary: Costs and Diagnostic Evidence**. The four result subsections follow four corresponding RQs: matched placement, equal-retention batching/export effects, offered load, and an illustrative localization task. The controlled cost figure is now included. |
| The small localization task cannot support a production discard headline. | RQ4 is explicitly illustrative in the question, abstract, methods, results, and conclusion. Its three handlers, single process, supplied candidates, chance baseline, constructed delays, real timing observations, and ideal offline selection are stated. Conditional discard values are examples in the results, not abstract or conclusion headlines. A new public-benchmark localizer evaluation is not claimed. |
| The bounds measure conditional replay uncertainty, not incident-population uncertainty. | The estimand is defined over a uniform draw from the fixed corpus and fresh selection randomness. The main text says what the confidence calculation covers. The full argument is in Appendix A; the main result is the connection between window size, retained reference evidence, and accuracy. No population interpretation is made. |
| Replace the calculation with exact enumeration and an incident bootstrap. | This recommendation was not adopted as stated. At 200 requests in each window, one incident has up to `2^400` head-selection masks; 600 observed incidents do not enumerate that space. Exact dynamic computation would require a separate verified algorithm. A bootstrap over this constructed, stratified corpus would require an explicit target population and resampling design and would not establish production validity. The frozen conditional procedure remains correct for its stated target and is now subordinate to the illustrative result. No post hoc primary estimator was substituted. |
| The early root marker and two-second wait may be unrealistic. | Delivery probes are supporting Appendix B material. Methods identify the imposed arrival order, two-second wait, five-second delay, cache levels, and joint churn/capacity factor. Background identifies the pinned processor's 30-second default. The test does not claim ordinary synchronous arrival order, production prevalence, or a response curve. |
| A root span always ends and exports last. | The OpenTelemetry API specification does not impose that general rule: children may remain active after their parent ends. This does not establish how frequently the probe order occurs. The paper distinguishes span lifetime from imposed Collector arrival order and makes no prevalence claim. |
| The paper reads as revision history. | The live perfect-baseline classifier, the withdrawn procedure, and historical anomaly narrative are absent from the active manuscript. Their observations and explanations remain in the technical supplement and preserved versions. Elementary results are background identities, not novelty claims; Lean stays in the artifact. |
| The abstract overgeneralizes costs. | It names the pinned Collector, shared node, loopback transport, JSON file boundary, and Collector process endpoint. The 82.3% and 2.9% statements concern matched trace IDs in that experiment. Methods and results state metadata and processor-work confounding. The cost model explicitly allows either sign for an in-Collector sampler. |
| The repeated 47.9% values appear mistaken. | Neither is a headline in the active paper. The technical supplement identifies their separate replay and live-data origins and opposite directions. Neither is substituted for the controlled placement or batching estimates. |
| Mint, UniSage, and Gleaner are missing. | Their versioned primary full texts were read and archived. Related work describes their different representations, timing, and utility objectives without claiming an experimental comparison. Uncited STEAM was removed. Metadata and version choices are recorded in `source-review.md`. |
| AI disclosure may need adjustment. | Methods now describe AI assistance in protocol design, literature work, programs, mathematical/proof code, validation, execution through tools, analysis, figures, and writing. Measurements are archived program observations. The end statement retains author responsibility. Venue rules and the access limitation on the publisher policy are recorded separately; no submission-compliance guarantee is made. |
| Length, figures, and operator guidance. | The requested article template and nine main sections are preserved. Three figures carry the cost, load, and evidence-window results; four tables remain. Discussion gives operational actions tied to measurements. A separate ACM layout estimate preserves the main content and does not replace the reader template. |
