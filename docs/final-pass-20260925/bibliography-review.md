## Errors to fix and precision improvements

No confirmed author, title, year, venue, pagination, or DOI error was found in the checked snapshot. The priority spellings and the 2021/2017 Sieve distinction are correct.

1. **Clarify Little's-law assumptions in `paper/theory.tex`.** “Stable, lossless” alone does not state all conditions in Little (1961). Its abstract requires finite means, strictly stationary processes, and a metrically transitive arrival process with nonzero mean. Use, for example, “under finite-mean stationary conditions and the arrival-process regularity required by Little's theorem.” Keep the existing statement that mean occupancy is not a hard burst bound. [Original publisher abstract](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/abs/10.1287/opre.9.3.383).
2. **Make source scope explicit for paired ratios.** NIST's paired-observation example concerns paired differences. The manuscript's Student-t interval over the defined ratios is a model-based one-sample interval on those block summaries. Its existing five-block/normality caveat is necessary; avoid claiming NIST validates the ratio distribution or exact coverage here. [NIST](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/prc/section3/prc311.htm).
3. **Add source citations if the new cost discussion enters the manuscript.** Existing sources do not directly document every batch/exporter setting. Use the versioned [batch README](https://github.com/open-telemetry/opentelemetry-collector/blob/v0.136.0/processor/batchprocessor/README.md), [file exporter source](https://github.com/open-telemetry/opentelemetry-collector-contrib/blob/v0.136.0/exporter/fileexporter/file_exporter.go), and [OTLP/HTTP exporter README](https://github.com/open-telemetry/opentelemetry-collector/blob/v0.136.0/exporter/otlphttpexporter/README.md) when making those mechanistic claims. This is a prospective citation addition, not a missing scientific work in the current snapshot.

Optional cleanup: remove the uncited Clopper-Pearson entry if it remains unused; remove the stale “intentionally omitted ... when merging” comment now that additions have been merged. Neither is a scientific metadata error. A stable JSTOR link can supplement Holm's university-hosted scan. Living sources already have access dates; preserving a dated source snapshot would improve longevity but should not be represented as completed unless actually archived.

## Access limitations

- IEEE's original Sieve 2021 full text and STEAM's publisher full text were unavailable through the checked routes. Metadata comes from publisher deposits and official records; content claims remain limited to the program/title or authors' abstract. Later papers' descriptions do not substitute for direct inspection of those originals.
- Direct DOI page requests for several ACM/JASA works sometimes failed, and the first parallel Crossref batch returned HTTP 429 for several records. Sequential metadata requests succeeded for every supplied DOI. TracePicker's publisher article was available through indexed primary content, including Section 5.1.2. No secondary summary was used to supply missing findings.
- The original Horvitz-Thompson, Wilson, and Hoeffding scans were located on university hosts, but their image-only pages were not fully text-extractable in this pass. Metadata was confirmed independently. The checked Wilson formula is explicitly available in NIST; the current paper's own expectation proof and stated Hoeffding assumptions should remain visible rather than implying this audit independently verified every line of the original proofs.
- The Little result was checked against its original publisher abstract, not a reconstructed broader theorem. The proposed assumption clarification is based on the conditions stated there.

This audit confirms bibliographic identity and bounded source alignment. It does not certify the experiment, establish novelty, or substitute for peer review.
