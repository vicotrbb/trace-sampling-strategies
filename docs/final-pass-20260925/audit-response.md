# Response to the final external audit

This revision treats the manuscript as an empirical measurement study of established sampling strategies. It preserves the main section order, author metadata, and reference LaTeX template. The prior delivery, its report, and its manifest are archived before changes. No publication or external peer review is claimed.

| Audit issue | Revision | Evidence and remaining boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Live diagnosis percentages follow the accepted baseline by construction. | Abstract no longer leads with those percentages. Introduction, methods, and results explicitly call them pipeline preservation checks. The fixed-classifier identity explains the expected aggregate; class-level allocation and partial-span interventions carry the empirical interpretation. | Full retention is an acceptance condition, every retained fault scores correctly, and the three known families are balanced. No new diagnostic algorithm or unfamiliar-fault challenge is claimed. |
| Collector CPU reverses direction between replay and live studies. | Both signs are reported together. The cost identity is applied to competing added decision work and avoided downstream work. All 96 cells of a separately frozen 2 x 2 x 3 intervention now pass archive validation. With dual export, retain-all tail changes from -0.173 CPU-seconds relative to bypass at 100 ms to +0.066 at 1,000 ms. | Actual batch counts establish the configuration change. All eighteen planned contrasts are retained. Historical totals alone cannot identify exporter cost, and the new conditional contrasts are not an intrinsic processor-cost decomposition. Post-collection JSON inspection also shows that preserved span content does not imply equal framing volume. |
| RQ4 certification was vacuous before collection. | Certification is withdrawn as an empirical contribution. Curves are descriptive; conditional mathematical thresholds remain.
