# Publication positioning after revision

Reassessed 28 September 2026. Earlier notes are preserved with the preceding manuscript. This is publication planning, not a submission or an acceptance forecast.

The manuscript is titled **Trace Sampling at the Collector Boundary: Costs and Diagnostic Evidence**. It centers on matched sampler placement crossed with export path, batching effects at equal span retention, and resource magnitude across offered load. The small localization task is an explicitly bounded illustration of evidence-window and policy dependence. Documented delivery mechanisms and a separate SDK resource comparison are in the main results. The elementary mathematics supplies assumptions and interpretation rather than a claimed new theory. There is no measured comparison with Mint, UniSage, Gleaner, or other utility-aware samplers.

## Format and dissemination

ICPE 2027 requires ten ACM double-column pages excluding references and appendices, and anonymized manuscripts and associated code. Appendices are not published, so essential reasoning must fit the main paper. Prior public release on arXiv is permitted subject to the anonymity instructions. These are submission-stage requirements, not evidence that the existing mathematics or measurements are invalid.
