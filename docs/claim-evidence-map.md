# Claim-to-evidence map

Current manuscript: **Trace Sampling at the Collector Boundary: Costs and Diagnostic Evidence**, 28 September 2026. Paths resolve from the artifact root. The validation report records completed checks for the delivered version.

| Manuscript claim | Evidence | Scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| RQ1: placement and export path change Collector cost at identical uniform trace selections. | Forty cells in `data/raw/fifth-audit-20260928/placement-confirm-v1/`; all group and paired interaction summaries in `data/derived/fifth-audit-20260928/placement-summary.json`. | Five randomized blocks at 40,000 offered spans/s, with JSON-only and JSON-plus-Jaeger controls in every block. Uniform selections match exactly; ingestion, processor work, and metadata are included. |
| Matching trace IDs does not ensure equal serialized volume. | Every retained span reconstructed; native tracestate checked separately; measured-phase JSON byte counts. | Complete uncompressed JSON representation. No compressed wire-byte inference. |
| Native uniform, tail, and pre-ingress savings have different measurement boundaries. | Matched input hashes, Collector and Jaeger process endpoints, their process sum, generator records, and pipeline configurations. | Pre-ingress hashing and input construction are excluded preparation. The generator quantity covers sending and monitoring only. The process sum is not total instrumentation or fleet cost. |
| RQ2: at low load, changing batching and export path reverses a retain-all tail CPU contrast. | Ninety-six cells and eight blocks in `data/raw/final-cost-20260925/`; all eighteen contrasts in the corresponding `cost-summary.json`. | Every paired dual-export block has lower tail CPU at 100 ms and higher CPU at 1,000 ms. This is a 250-span/s mechanism experiment. |
| RQ2: the higher-load contrast tests whether that behavior persists at a new operating point. | Twenty cells and five blocks in `data/raw/fifth-audit-20260928/batching-confirm-v2/`; both timeouts and their paired interaction in `batching-summary.json`. | 5,000 offered spans/s with identical SDK-template input across treatments. Capacity and identification attributes also differ from the older campaign, so cross-rate comparison does not isolate offered load alone. Failed measured and pilot attempts remain excluded and archived. |
| Equal span content can have different batch formation, resource/scope grouping, JSON volume, and CPU. | Batch size/timeout counters, complete JSON exports, old serialization inspection, and pinned tail, batch, and file-exporter implementations. `docs/final-publication-20260928/batch-mechanism-validation.json` reconstructs all six million measured spans in the higher-load cells. | Bypass forms 1,000 batches of 300 spans. Tail at 100 ms forms 1,139 batches of 260 plus 61 partial batches; at 1,000 ms it forms 1,153 batches of 260 plus one of 220. The 256-span target is a trigger, not an exact size. |
| RQ3: reduced output can coexist with more Collector CPU and memory at higher offered load. | Eighty cells in `data/raw/second-audit-20260925/load-confirm-v1/`; sixteen treatment summaries and twelve paired contrasts. | Four offered rates and five blocks at the Collector-plus-JSON boundary. Its BLAKE2b gate differs from the matched native-hash gate. No saturation or monetary-cost claim. |
| An SDK decision has a separately observed application resource effect. | Sixty live cells in `data/raw/revision-20260924/confirm-v1/`; restored head/full rows from `data/derived/revision/live-resources.json`, rechecked against raw process ticks. | Checkout/documents, steady/bursty, fifty requests/s. Application CPU sums gateway and worker. These resource results do not validate the earlier known-family classifier as general diagnosis. |
| RQ4: window size and policy affect the evidence and accuracy of a fixed localizer. | Six hundred observed incidents, nested windows, separate healthy calibration, all 240,000 replay rows, and `data/derived/fifth-audit-20260928/localization-descriptive.json`. | Three handlers, known incident boundaries and candidates, measured HTTP timings, ideal offline sampling, and all full-data errors. | Calibrated tail changes both reference/incident allocation and retained duration distributions. | All forty-two feasible configurations, per-trial counts, accuracy, losses, and gains; the current evidence-window figure. | Corpus-wide expected-budget matching. | Standard errors describe conditional Monte Carlo precision, including paired gains. | `scripts/verify_fifth_context.py` recomputes 84 standard errors from the raw replay rows using sample standard deviations. | Replays add simulation precision, not observed incidents. No largest-qualifying-discard, future-population, or subgroup guarantee. Former bounds remain in the artifact. |
| Witness availability, protected-budget allocation, and window counts have explicit mathematical laws. | Main reference equations and `proofs/reference-results.tex`, included as Appendix A in the preprint and a separate anonymous supplement in the review package; retained computational checks and six restricted Lean statements. | Standard identities under stated assumptions. | Decision, cache, and capacity mechanisms can destroy a stipulated complete witness. | Forty-eight matched probes and the separate burst probe, full exports and counters; grouped main-text table. | Immediate/five-second arrival relative to a two-second wait. | The artifact preserves the observed record and supports internal reconstruction. | Four prior and two new measured-execution freezes, prior raw-file hashes, complete new exports, failed measured and excluded pilot attempts, separate validators, corruption controls, input-hash reconstruction, and PDF review. | Internal AI-assisted workflow. No external replication, authenticated preregistration, public deposit, DOI, or peer-review acceptance is claimed. |

The older twenty-cell JSON placement study, live-classifier curves, selected hash anomaly, historical statistical designs, and earlier fixed-window corpus remain in the artifact and technical supplement. The two localization corpora and separate resource campaigns are not pooled. The current title, original template, author, nine main sections, and conclusion are preserved.

These paths describe the complete research archive. The compact anonymous review ZIP contains a documented subset: CPU endpoints and recorded results, relevant derived summaries, all localization replay outcomes, and matched delivery-probe inputs/exports. Its numerical checks do not replace the complete archive's payload-hash, backend-delivery, large span-export, calibration or timing-corpus validation. The package README states its coverage explicitly.
