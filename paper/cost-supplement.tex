\subsection{Complete controlled cost summaries}\label{app:cost}
Table~\ref{tab:cost-contrasts} gives every primary paired CPU contrast. Table~\ref{tab:cost-batches} reports all twelve factor combinations, including Jaeger CPU in file-only cells where the process runs without receiving study traces. Table~\ref{tab:cost-interactions} reports every planned interaction. Batch counts and sizes are measured counter differences, not values inferred from the timeout setting. CPU ticks resolve 0.01\,s; additional decimal places in means and intervals reflect arithmetic across eight blocks. The accompanying \artifact{cost-summary.json} and \artifact{cost-cells.csv} retain every block value.
